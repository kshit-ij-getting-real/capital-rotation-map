India Equities Theme Tracker [EWT]

Identify where the "Smart Money" is flowing in the Indian Markets. The Indian Equities Theme Tracker [EWT] is a powerful treemap heatmap and table dashboard built for NSE traders and investors to monitor sector rotation and relative strength in near-real-time. By tracking the most liquid Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) and supporting sector-to-stock mapping for the NIFTY 500 universe, this tool provides a birds-eye view of the Indian economy—from core benchmarks like Nifty 50 and Nifty 500 to high-growth themes like Defence, EV, Tourism, and Energy. In modern markets, capital does not move into all stocks at once; it rotates between sectors. This dashboard helps you spot leaders and laggards across five timeframes so you can stay aligned with the strongest themes.

Key Features:
- 23+ Essential Themes: Tracks broad market benchmarks, market-cap segments, sectors, and narratives.
- Dynamic Performance Sorting: Automatically reorders based on your selected lookback (1 Day, 1 Week, 1 Month, 3 Months, or YTD).
- Heatmap Logic: Intuitive color coding to identify extreme bullishness or bearishness.
- Liquidity Focused: Uses the most liquid NSE ETFs (BeES and equivalent) to reflect tradeable prices.
- Real-Time Provider Abstraction: Uses a preferred real-time feed when API credentials are present, with Yahoo/Stooq-style fallback and delayed-data badge when fallback is active.
- Pro UI Design: Clean dashboard with treemap heatmap and optional sortable table view.
- Two-Line Labels: Every tile shows ticker on line one and full name in parentheses on line two, with graceful truncation.

Themes Included:
- Benchmarks: Nifty 500, Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50.
- Market Caps: Midcap 150, Smallcap 250.
- Sectors: Private & PSU Banks, IT, Pharma, Healthcare, FMCG, Auto, Metals, Infra, Realty.
- Thematic/Narratives: Defence, Tourism, Energy, EV & New Age Automotive, Consumption.
- Safe Havens: Gold & Silver.

How to use:
- Timeframe: Daily map view is recommended for quick rotation reads.
- Settings: Adjust sorting/timeframe and size metric, choose ETF or sector→stock mode, and use search to find tickers.
- Strategy: Focus on themes leading in 1 Month and 3 Months for structural strength, and use 1 Day for tactical moves.
